What we do

N2O Music Group provides distribution and publishing administration for independent artists, producers, and labels.

We offer two paths:

  • Guided Release: hands-on support for artists building their first serious release cycle, covering metadata, splits, DSP delivery, and rollout strategy.
  • Label Services: full distribution and publishing infrastructure for established independents who want label-level reach without a label deal.

Our terms are built to protect artists. You keep your masters, you see your numbers, and you’re never locked in longer than makes sense.

We also handle publishing administration: registrations, royalty collection, and making sure your songwriting income actually reaches you.
